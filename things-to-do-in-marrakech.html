Things to Do in Marrakech: 12 Experiences Worth Your Time

Marrakech is a city of contrasts: busy souks, quiet courtyards, historic monuments and neighbourhood life. If it is your first visit, start with the medina and give yourself time to explore beyond the main square.

By Chafik Haqqoum · Licensed Local Tour Guide · Updated 26 November 2026

1. Explore the historic Medina

The old city is the heart of Marrakech. Wander its lanes, notice the architecture and look beyond the busiest streets to discover neighbourhood bakeries, workshops and everyday life.

2. Visit Jemaa el-Fna

The city’s famous square changes throughout the day. Visit in daylight for its atmosphere, then return in the evening when food stalls and performers bring a different energy.

3. Discover the souks

The covered markets are full of leather goods, lamps, textiles, spices and traditional crafts. Take your time, compare quality and enjoy meeting the artisans.

4. See the historic monuments

Add Ben Youssef Madrasa, Bahia Palace, the Saadian Tombs and El Badi Palace to your list. Each offers a different window into the city’s history and craftsmanship.

5. Find a garden

Majorelle Garden is the best-known, while Le Jardin Secret offers a calmer pause within the medina. Check current ticket arrangements before visiting.

6. Taste Moroccan food

Try a tagine, couscous, harira, pastilla, fresh bread and mint tea. Food is one of the easiest ways to connect with local culture.

7. Experience a traditional hammam

A hammam is a traditional bathing ritual and an important part of local life. Choose a reputable establishment and check what the treatment includes.

8. Explore the Mellah

The historic Jewish quarter has its own character, markets and heritage. It is a rewarding area for visitors interested in the many layers of Marrakech.

9. Look for artisan workshops

Visit areas where metalworkers, leatherworkers, dyers and woodworkers practise their crafts. A little context makes these encounters much more meaningful.

10. Enjoy a rooftop view

A rooftop café is a lovely place to pause and watch the medina from above, especially around sunset.

11. Take a day trip

The Atlas Mountains, Ourika Valley, Agafay and Essaouira offer very different escapes from the city. Choose according to your available time and interests.

12. Walk with a local guide

A guided walk can help you understand the city’s history, navigate the medina and discover details you might otherwise pass by.
