Marrakech Itinerary: How to Spend 3 Days in the Red City

Three days is a comfortable amount of time for a first visit to Marrakech. It gives you room to explore the medina, visit a few major sights, enjoy Moroccan food and choose whether to spend your final day in the city or outside it.

By Chafik Haqqoum · Licensed Local Tour Guide · Updated 26 November 2026

Day 1: Get to know the Medina

Start around Jemaa el-Fna, then explore the northern souks and the lanes around Mouassine. Visit Ben Youssef Madrasa if it fits your interests, and take a relaxed rooftop break. In the evening, return to the square for its changing atmosphere.

Day 2: Discover the southern historic quarter

Visit Bahia Palace and explore the Mellah and surrounding streets. Depending on your pace, add El Badi Palace or the Saadian Tombs. Leave time for a long lunch and a stroll through the Kasbah area.

Day 3: Choose your own pace

Spend the morning at Majorelle Garden or Le Jardin Secret, browse a craft area you missed, or take a food experience. If you prefer to leave the city, consider the Ourika Valley, Atlas Mountains, Agafay or Essaouira—remember that Essaouira is a longer day.

A few planning tips

Book timed-entry attractions in advance where required, group nearby sights together and avoid scheduling too much during the hottest hours. Keep some space in your plan for unexpected discoveries.

Should you hire a guide?

A local guide can be especially helpful on your first day, when the medina’s layout and history are new. You can then revisit favourite areas independently.
