Marrakech Souks: A Local Guide to Markets, Crafts and Shopping

The souks of Marrakech are not one single market but a network of specialised streets and small trading areas. You will find lamps, leather, textiles, carpets, spices, metalwork and workshops where craftspeople still work by hand.

By Chafik Haqqoum · Licensed Local Tour Guide · Updated 26 November 2026

What can you find in the souks?

Different areas are known for different crafts. Look for leather goods and babouches, woven textiles, lanterns, woodwork, pottery, spices and traditional clothing.

Watch the artisans at work

Some of the most interesting places are workshops rather than shops. You may see wool being dyed, metal shaped, leather prepared or lamps assembled. Be respectful when taking photographs and ask first.

How to navigate the markets

The lanes can feel confusing at first. Use a recognisable landmark such as Jemaa el-Fna or the Koutoubia as a reference, and save your riad’s location on your phone.

How to bargain respectfully

Bargaining is part of shopping in many souks. Keep it friendly, decide what an item is worth to you and be prepared to walk away politely. There is no need to turn every purchase into a contest.

How to choose quality

Check stitching, material, finish and weight. For carpets or leather goods, ask about the materials and how the item was made. Compare a few shops before deciding.

When should you visit?

Late morning and afternoon are lively, while quieter moments can make it easier to browse. Some workshops and shops keep different schedules, especially around prayer times and holidays.
