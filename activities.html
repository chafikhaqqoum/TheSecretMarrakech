Moroccan Cooking Class

Discover the flavours of Moroccan home cooking in a local riad kitchen. Learn about familiar ingredients, aromatic spices and traditional preparation before sitting down to enjoy the dishes made during the class.

  • A private, hands-on cooking experience
  • Introduction to Moroccan ingredients and spices
  • Prepare traditional dishes with guidance
  • Enjoy the meal you have prepared
DurationApproximately 4 hours
PriceFrom €45 per person
A Day Beyond the City

Atlas Mountains Day Trip

Spend a day beyond the city and discover the foothills of the High Atlas, mountain scenery and traditional villages. The route and pace can be discussed when enquiring so the day suits your interests.

  • Discover mountain landscapes and village life
  • Enjoy scenic stops along the route
  • Optional gentle walking, depending on the itinerary
  • Ask about private arrangements and inclusions
DurationFull day
PriceFrom €60 per person
