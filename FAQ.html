FAQ

Marrakech tours — your questions answered.

Practical answers about guided medina walks, souk and food tours, Atlas Mountains and Agafay desert day trips, prices, timings and what to expect in Marrakech.

What is the best guided tour to do on a first trip to Marrakech?

Most first-time visitors start with a private walking tour of the Marrakech medina: the souks, the tanneries area, Jemaa el-Fnaa square and the historic quarters around Bahia Palace and the Saadian Tombs. It gives you your bearings in a few hours, so the rest of your stay is far easier to navigate on your own.

How long does a medina and souk tour take?

A half-day walk is usually three to four hours, at a relaxed pace with tea and photo stops. A full day of roughly six to seven hours adds the palaces, the Jewish quarter (Mellah), a food stop and time in the quieter craft workshops away from the main souk lanes.

Are the tours private or in a group?

Every tour is private — just you, your family or your friends. Nothing is shared with strangers, and the route is adapted to your pace, your interests and the time you have.

How much does a private guide in Marrakech cost?

Prices depend on the length of the day, the number of people and whether transport or entrance tickets are included. Send me your dates and what you'd like to see and I'll reply with a clear, all-in price before you commit — there are no hidden extras and no shop commissions.

Can you organise day trips from Marrakech?

Yes. The most popular are the Atlas Mountains and the Ourika Valley, the Agafay desert for sunset and dinner, Essaouira on the Atlantic coast, and the Ouzoud waterfalls. All are done as private day trips with an air-conditioned vehicle and a driver, returning to Marrakech the same evening.

What should I wear for a tour in Marrakech?

Comfortable closed shoes for the medina's uneven lanes, and clothing that covers shoulders and knees when visiting religious or historic sites. Bring a hat, sunglasses and water in summer — from June to September the afternoons are very hot, so we usually start early.

Is Marrakech safe for tourists, and for solo women travellers?

Marrakech is generally safe, and violent crime against visitors is rare. The usual annoyances are unofficial guides, aggressive sellers and getting lost in the souks. With a licensed guide those disappear, and many solo women travellers book precisely so that first day feels calm and comfortable.

Will I be taken to shops where you earn commission?

No. I never take clients on forced shopping stops. If you want to buy rugs, spices, leather or lanterns I'll show you honest workshops and help you understand fair prices, but buying is always entirely your choice.

What languages do you speak?

Tours are guided in English, French and Arabic (Moroccan Darija). Tell me your preference when you book.

When is the best time of year to visit Marrakech?

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the most comfortable, with warm days and cool evenings. Winter is mild and pleasant for walking, with cold nights and snow visible on the Atlas. July and August are very hot, so tours run early morning or late afternoon.

How far in advance should I book?

A week or two ahead is ideal, especially over Easter, Christmas, New Year and Moroccan holidays. Last-minute requests are often still possible — message me on WhatsApp and I'll tell you straight away what's free.

Do you pick up from riads and hotels?

Yes. I meet you at your riad, hotel or the nearest accessible square in the medina, and for day trips the vehicle collects you from your accommodation.
