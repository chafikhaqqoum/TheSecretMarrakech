FAQ

Marrakech tours — your questions answered.

Practical answers about guided medina walks, souk and food tours, Atlas Mountains and Agafay desert day trips, prices, timings and what to expect in Marrakech.

What is the best guided tour to do on a first trip to Marrakech?

Most first-time visitors start with a private walking tour of the Marrakech medina: the souks, the tanneries area, Jemaa el-Fnaa square and the historic quarters around Bahia Palace and the Saadian Tombs.

How long does a medina and souk tour take?

A half-day walk is usually three to four hours, at a relaxed pace with tea and photo stops.

Are the tours private or in a group?

Every tour is offered in two formats: small public groups or fully private tours tailored to your pace.

How much does a private guide in Marrakech cost?

Prices depend on length and group size. Send your dates for a clear, all-in quote with zero hidden extras.
