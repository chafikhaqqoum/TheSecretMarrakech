Agafay Desert — Sunset & Dinner

Leave the city behind for an evening in the Agafay desert: a camel ride at sunset with the Atlas Mountains on the horizon, mint tea, and a three-course Moroccan dinner around a campfire under the stars.

  • Round-trip transport from your hotel
  • Camel ride at sunset over the rocky dunes
  • Mint tea & three-course Moroccan dinner (Harira, salad, tajine)
  • Campfire, Berber music, and a sky full of stars
